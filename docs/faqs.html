Countify+ FAQ

Where writing meets precision.

What is Countify+?

Countify+ is your personal writing companion — a smart, offline-ready text manager that helps you track, organize, and understand your writing. It counts words, analyzes your writing patterns, supports voice input, and keeps everything automatically saved — all while protecting your privacy.

What makes Countify+ different from other writing tools?

Unlike most web apps, Countify+ works fully offline and stores everything locally on your device. It's designed with focus, speed, and privacy in mind — no logins, no cloud storage, no distractions. Every feature, from intelligent word analytics to real-time saving, was crafted to help you write better, not just faster.

How can I start using Countify+ efficiently?

Just open Countify+ and start typing. Your text is automatically saved every three seconds.

Here are quick tips to make the most out of it:

  • Use Activities to separate projects or sessions.
  • Check Analytics to monitor your productivity and writing time.
  • Try Voice Dictation when your ideas come faster than your fingers.
  • Use Auto-Formatting to instantly clean and correct text.
  • Explore Themes for your preferred writing atmosphere — light or dark.

How does the auto-save system work?

Countify+ saves automatically every three seconds, indicated by a subtle visual cue.

If the text field is empty, Countify+ won't create unnecessary files.

Your latest work is always stored safely in one single local file — preventing duplicates and ensuring peace of mind.

Can I use Countify+ without an internet connection?

Absolutely. Countify+ is built to function completely offline. Every file, setting, and analytics record is stored in your browser's local storage, keeping your work private and accessible anytime — even without Wi-Fi.

What can I do with the Voice & Input features?

Countify+ lets you write using just your voice through real-time Speech-to-Text Dictation. With a single tap, you can start, pause, stop, or add spaces manually using on-screen buttons.

The Intelligent Punctuation (Puncify Beta) automatically detects where commas, periods, and other punctuation marks should appear, keeping your sentences clean and natural as you speak.

Watch your words form live through the Voice Visualizer, an animated display that makes voice writing smooth, responsive, and enjoyable.

How do I organize my writing activities?

Your writings are stored as Activities.

Each one can be:

  • Viewed under Today, Yesterday, or All Activities.
  • Duplicated for quick editing.
  • Archived for safekeeping.
  • Deleted and later restored from the Recycle Bin.

Countify+ even gives smart default titles like Morning Note or Evening Draft to help you stay organized without extra effort.

How do I search for my past writings?

Use the Full-Screen Search tool for instant access to any text you've written.

Type a word or phrase, and Countify+ filters results in real time.

It's designed for large text libraries — fast, accurate, and distraction-free.

How do I clean or format my text?

Countify+ includes smart Text Cleaning tools:

  • Remove extra spaces
  • Change case (UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title, Sentence)
  • Reverse text for fun or testing

You can also use Auto-Formatting to tidy your work instantly — perfect for writers, editors, or students.

Can I undo mistakes while editing?

Yes. The Undo/Redo system supports up to 50 history steps, so you can go back or forward with confidence. The buttons visually respond to your actions, giving you control at every move.

What types of files can I import or export?

Countify+ supports both .txt and .md (Markdown) files.

You can also copy and paste text directly — Countify+ automatically removes formatting to keep everything clean and consistent.

How does Countify+ protect my data?

Your privacy is non-negotiable.

  • All your data stays locally on your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded or shared online.
  • Temporary data is automatically cleaned to maintain performance.
  • LanLink (beta) transfers are digitally verified to ensure security.

Countify+ is built for trust — not tracking.

What is LanLink (beta) Integration?

LanLink (beta) allows secure, one-tap text transfers between Countify+ and LanWord, your advanced text editor.

It's ideal for users who work across multiple LanDecs tools, ensuring smooth data flow with zero cloud involvement.

What are the productivity and analytics features?

Countify+ helps you visualize your progress:

  • Writing History graph shows your daily word output.
  • Productivity Stats track your most active days and sessions.
  • Writing Streaks motivate you to write every day.
  • Performance Metrics summarize your average speed and total time spent.

These analytics give you a clear picture of your growth — so you can keep improving with every session.

Can I customize the interface?

Definitely. Countify+ gives you full control:

  • Switch between Dark and Light Mode instantly.
  • Enable or disable LanLink and Puncify features.
  • Enjoy smooth ripple animations, haptic feedback, and a glass-morphic UI designed to feel fluid and modern.

Your workspace adapts to your rhythm — not the other way around.

Are there any keyboard shortcuts?

Yes — Countify+ supports essential shortcuts for faster writing:

  • Ctrl + S → Save manually
  • Ctrl + Z / Ctrl + Y → Undo / Redo
  • Ctrl + F → Quick search

Perfect for users who prefer precision through the keyboard.

Can Countify+ run on any device?

Yes. Countify+ is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — it runs seamlessly on all modern browsers, desktop or mobile. You can even install it like an app for instant access, no store downloads required.

What is LanLink (Beta)?

LanLink (Beta) is an upcoming integration system designed to connect Countify+ with other LanDecs apps — such as LanWord — for a smoother and smarter writing experience. It allows users to securely transfer their text between apps, enabling a connected workspace within the LanDecs ecosystem.

While it's currently in Beta, meaning it's not yet fully functional or stable, its presence in Countify+ shows that development is actively ongoing. The feature is being refined to ensure security, reliability, and seamless cross-app communication once officially released.
