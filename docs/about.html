Our Story

From vision to reality

Created with care by LanDecs, Countify+ was born from a vision: to make writing effortless, inspiring focus, growth, and confidence with every word.

emoji_objects The Beginning

Countify+ began in 2022 as a personal project: a writing tool as natural as pen and paper but powered by modern tech, solving issues like intrusive ads and tedious word counting. 2023 brought a major redesign and new features. 2024 marked its public release, and 2025 introduced a complete update, making it smarter, faster, and more user-focused than ever.

trending_up The Journey

From a simple personal tool in 2022 to a public release in 2024, Countify+ has grown through major redesigns and user feedback into a powerful, intuitive writing companion that transforms the way you create, track, and analyze text.
